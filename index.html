☀️ AM Training Schedule — Built for Busy Days
Train at 6am before work and caregiving pull you in every direction. Three solid meals. No skipped food windows. Done and done.
🏋 Training Day — Mon / Wed / Fri
6:45 am — Post-Workout Breakfast ⭐
Recovery Breakfast
• 4 eggs scrambled (5 min in microwave or pan)
• 1 cup full-fat Greek yogurt
• ½ cup mixed berries
• 1 slice whole wheat toast
P: 52g C: 45g F: 22g 580 cal
12:00 pm — Lunch (prep-ahead)
Power Plate
• 6 oz rotisserie chicken breast (shredded)
• 1 microwaveable brown rice pouch (90 sec)
• 2 cups pre-washed salad greens
• 1 tbsp olive oil + squeeze of lemon
P: 50g C: 50g F: 20g 580 cal
6:00 pm — Dinner
Salmon & Sides
• 1 pouched salmon (Wild Planet) or 2 cans tuna
• 1 medium sweet potato (microwave 5 min)
• 1 bag steam-in-bag broccoli or green beans
• ½ avocado • hot sauce / lemon
P: 42g C: 45g F: 30g 600 cal
Daily Total: P: 144g C: 140g F: 72g ~1,760 cal
+ 1 scoop protein shake or snack to close the gap to 2,650 cal target
🏃 Cardio / Rest Day — Tue / Thu / Sat / Sun
7:00 am — Breakfast
Morning Fuel
• 4 eggs scrambled
• 1 cup full-fat Greek yogurt
• ½ cup berries
No toast on rest days — slightly lower carbs
P: 52g C: 25g F: 22g 490 cal
12:00 pm — Lunch (same as training day)
Power Plate
• 6 oz rotisserie chicken breast
• 1 cup microwaveable brown rice
• 2 cups pre-washed salad greens
• 1 tbsp olive oil + lemon

Tuna & Greens

Skip sweet potato on rest days

📅 Sunday Batch Cook — 1 Hour, All Week Covered
You don’t have time to cook every day — and that’s okay. One focused hour on Sunday sets up 5 lunch-ready containers. Breakfast is 5 minutes. Dinner pulls from what’s already prepped.
TaskTimeWhat you get
Buy 1 rotisserie chicken 0 min cook Pull it apart into 5 lunch containers (6 oz each). Done.
Cook rice pouches × 5 90 sec each Portion into lunch containers with chicken. Refrigerates all week.
Hard-boil 10 eggs 12 min Backup protein. Grab 2 anytime. No cooking required mid-week.
Stock the fridge 5 min Greek yogurt, eggs, berries, pre-washed greens, tuna pouches. That’s it.
Microwave 3 sweet potatoes 5 min each Reheat in 60 sec any night this week. Training day dinners covered.
✓ Weekday routine (5 min or less per meal):
Breakfast: Scramble 4 eggs • scoop yogurt • grab berries • done in 5 min.
Lunch: Pull container from fridge. Eat cold or 90-sec microwave.
Dinner: Tuna pouch + bagged salad + reheat sweet potato • 5 min total.
